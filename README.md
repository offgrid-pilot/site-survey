# OGP site survey

Paragliding site survey for Offgrid Pilot testers: https://offgrid-pilot.github.io/site-survey/

Pilots pick the sites they fly, answer what they know and email their answers in. Answers stay on the pilot's phone until they send them; this page stores nothing.

The page is generated: the source is `docs/sites/survey/site-survey.html` in the app repo. Change it there, then copy it here (wrapped in a doctype and head) and push.
